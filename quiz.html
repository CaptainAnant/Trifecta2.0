Q1: Global Heatwave Alert!

The last 8 years have been the hottest on record globally. What is the primary cause?

💡 Reasoning: Greenhouse gases trap heat in the atmosphere, causing global temperatures to rise. Natural cycles or volcanic activity contribute minimally compared to human emissions.

Q2: Scenario: The Ghost Chill 👻

A college cafeteria has its ACs running at full blast even when the space is empty. Why might this be happening, and what would be the best action to take?

💡 Reasoning: Timers reduce unnecessary energy use while maintaining comfort. It’s a practical, scalable solution rather than ignoring or taking extreme measures.

Q3: Case Study: Odd-Even Obstacle

Your city introduced an odd-even vehicle rule to reduce emissions. Many students complained it was “inconvenient.” What’s the best way to improve student participation?

💡 Reasoning: Shuttle buses make the odd-even rule convenient, encouraging participation. Banning vehicles or exemptions would reduce compliance and effectiveness.

Q4: Carbon Footprint Culprit!

Which of these is the biggest contributor to an individual’s carbon footprint?

💡 Reasoning: Air travel emits large amounts of CO₂ per passenger, far more than daily water or electricity use. It’s the biggest contributor to an individual’s carbon footprint.

Q6: Case Study: Water Mission Challenge

The National Water Mission aims for 20% water-use efficiency. Challenges persist: urban areas face massive pipeline leakages, while rural communities overuse groundwater. As the head of a district climate task force, what initiative would create the most long-term impact?

💡 Reasoning: This addresses both urban wastage (metering) and rural overuse/replenishment (rainwater harvesting) with practical, technology-driven solutions for long-term resilience.
